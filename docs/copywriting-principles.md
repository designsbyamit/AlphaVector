# AlphaVector copywriting principles

Set by Amit. They apply to the site, the app and anything we send.

1. **Talk to designers, not about designers.** Use "you", "your work", "your next move".
2. **Avoid sounding like an assessment tool.** The experience should feel like discovery, not an examination. No scores, grades, "fit", "assess" or "analysis" in headlines.
3. **Do not promise to find someone's calling.** Help them uncover possibilities and make informed choices. Words to avoid: calling, destiny, purpose-finding, "your true path".
4. **Make intelligence approachable.** There is sophisticated analysis behind the experience, and the language should be effortless. Detail goes behind a disclosure or the technical note.
5. **Encourage action, not just introspection.** The outcome is a direction and a practical way forward.

## The brand line

**Your next move deserves more than a guess.**

Supporting line: Your design career. Less guesswork. More direction.

Call to action: **Find your direction.**

## Use and avoid

| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| direction, possibilities, next move, clues, sweet spot | calling, fix, score, fit, assessment, framework-speak |
| "your strengths", "what energises you", "where the industry is heading" | "Strengths dimension", "Passion signal" |
| "Bring your projects. We will connect the dots." | "Complete the questionnaire" |

## Claims we can and cannot make today

- **"More than about 80 percent of designers decide on guesswork and gut feel":** not published. It needs a source or our own survey first. The line "Your next move deserves more than a guess" works without the number.
- **"No sign-up needed":** true for running the analysis. Saving a Blueprint needs an account, so say "no sign-up needed to start".
- **Imports:** the app accepts typed projects, a CV, a spreadsheet, a portfolio link or a pasted list. It does not read LinkedIn profiles, so do not say it does.
- **Testimonials:** only real, consented quotes, added in `lib/testimonials.ts`. The section stays hidden until then.
- **Algorithms:** weights, formulas, distance bands and source ratings are not published. They are shared on request through the technical note.
- **For designers, for now:** the skills, industries and routes are drawn from design careers. Say so plainly.
